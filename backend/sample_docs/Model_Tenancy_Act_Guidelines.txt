MINISTRY OF HOUSING AND URBAN AFFAIRS (MoHUA) — GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
MODEL TENANCY ACT & STATE TENANCY HARMONIZATION GUIDELINES
Subject: Statutory Rights of Tenants, Security Deposit Caps, and Protection from Arbitrary Eviction

1. MANDATORY STATUTORY CAP ON SECURITY DEPOSIT (SECTION 11):
- Section 11(1) of Model Tenancy Act explicitly caps security deposit:
  a. For Residential Premises: The security deposit to be paid by the tenant in advance shall not exceed a maximum of TWO (2) MONTHS' RENT.
  b. For Non-Residential / Commercial Premises: Maximum of SIX (6) MONTHS' RENT.
- Any lease clause demanding 6 to 10 months deposit for residential premises is contrary to statutory provisions and legally unenforceable.
- The security deposit shall be refunded to the tenant on the date of handing over vacant possession, after deducting legitimate unpaid rent or utility dues only.

2. STATUTORY PROTECTION FROM UNFAIR EVICTION & ESSENTIAL SERVICES (SECTION 20 & 21):
- Prohibition of Cutting Off Essential Services (Section 20): No landlord shall, either by himself or through any person, withhold or cut off any essential supply or service (water, electricity, sewerage, elevator, common access) to the tenant's premises. Penalty of up to ₹50,000 or 1 month rent compensation for violation.
- Lawful Eviction Procedure (Section 21): A landlord CANNOT evict a tenant arbitrarily or by force or with short notice. Eviction can only be ordered by the Rent Court on specific statutory grounds:
  a. Willful default of rent for 2 consecutive months despite notice.
  b. Subletting without written consent.
  c. Misuse of premises causing public nuisance or structural damage.
- Notice Period: Landlord must serve a minimum written notice of 90 days before the expiry of the tenancy agreement if non-renewal is intended.

2A. MANDATORY ADVANCE NOTICE FOR INSPECTION OR ENTRY (STATUTORY RULE):
- Under the Model Tenancy Act rules (Entry into premises), the landlord or property manager must give at least TWENTY-FOUR (24) HOURS' NOTICE in writing or electronically before entering the premises for carrying out inspection or repairs.
- Permitted Entry Hours: Entry is permitted only between 7:00 AM and 8:00 PM (except in emergency situations such as fire or major hazards).

3. DIVISION OF MAINTENANCE & REPAIRS (SCHEDULE II):
- Landlord's Responsibility: Structural repairs, major seepage/roof leakage, external painting, electrical wiring overhaul, water pump motor replacement.
- Tenant's Responsibility: Routine replacement of light bulbs, internal tap washers, kitchen drain unblocking, and minor routine cleaning.
- Arbitrary deductions from security deposit for "painting charges" or "building depreciation" without proven tenant damage are strictly prohibited.

4. DISPUTE RESOLUTION MECHANISM:
- Aggrieved tenants can submit an online or physical application before the Rent Authority (Deputy Collector / Sub-Divisional Magistrate).
- The Rent Authority is statutorily mandated to dispose of petitions within SIXTY (60) DAYS.
