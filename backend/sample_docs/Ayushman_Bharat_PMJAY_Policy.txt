GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
MINISTRY OF HEALTH AND FAMILY WELFARE
NATIONAL HEALTH AUTHORITY (NHA)
Notification Ref: NHA/AB-PMJAY/2024/CIRCULAR-12

OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES: AYUSHMAN BHARAT PRADHAN MANTRI JAN AROGYA YOJANA (AB-PMJAY)

1. OBJECTIVES AND OVERVIEW
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is the world's largest government-funded health assurance scheme. The scheme is designed to provide financial protection against catastrophic health expenditures and to reduce out-of-pocket expenditure for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization across India.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
(a) Deprivation Criteria (Rural): Families identified under the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC 2011) database belonging to D1, D2, D3, D4, D5, and D7 categories (families with only one room, kuccha walls/roof, female-headed households with no adult male member, SC/ST households, landless manual casual labor households).
(b) Occupational Criteria (Urban): 11 occupational categories including street vendors, domestic workers, sanitation workers, transport workers, and construction workers.
(c) Universal Senior Citizen Extension (2024 Amendment): All senior citizens aged 70 years and above, irrespective of family income or economic status, are eligible for a dedicated top-up health cover under the Ayushman Vaya Vandana Card.

3. EXCLUSION CRITERIA
(a) Families owning motorized 2/3/4-wheelers or motorized fishing boats.
(b) Families owning mechanized 3/4-wheeler agricultural equipment.
(c) Government employees or families with members employed in government service.
(d) Families paying professional tax or owning enterprise registered with the government.
(e) Families paying income tax.

4. HEALTH ASSURANCE BENEFITS
(a) Comprehensive cashless and paperless health insurance cover of Rs. 5,00,000/- per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization.
(b) No restriction on family size, age, or gender.
(c) Pre-existing conditions are covered from day one of scheme enrollment.
(d) Covers up to 3 days of pre-hospitalization and 15 days of post-hospitalization expenses, including diagnostic tests and medications.
(e) Over 1,949 treatment procedures across 27 specialties (cardiology, oncology, neurosurgery, orthopedics) are covered under Standard Health Benefit Packages (HBP).

5. MANDATORY DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR AYUSHMAN CARD
(a) Aadhaar Card of the beneficiary (mandatory for biometric identity verification).
(b) Ration Card or Family Identification Document (Samagra ID, Parivar Pehchan Patra, etc.).
(c) Active Mobile number for OTP validation and Ayushman Bharat Health Account (ABHA) creation.

6. HOW TO ACCESS TREATMENT AND APPLY
(a) Ayushman Mitras: Stationed at helpdesks in all empanelled public and private hospitals (EHCPs) to assist patients with card generation and admission.
(b) Beneficiary Identification System (BIS) Portal: https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in
(c) Portability: Beneficiaries can avail cashless treatment at any empanelled hospital across any State or Union Territory in India.

7. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL AND HELPLINES

(c) Grievance Portal: https://grievance.pmjay.gov.in
